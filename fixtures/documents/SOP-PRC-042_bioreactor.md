# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
**Document ID:** SOP-PRC-042  
**Version:** 3.1  
**Effective Date:** 2025-06-01  
**Title:** Upstream Mammalian Cell Culture & Bioreactor Operational Controls  
**Site:** Global Biomanufacturing Operations (SITE-SF-01)  

---

### 1.0 Purpose
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) defines the operational parameters, monitoring limits, and critical process parameters (CPPs) for 2000L single-use bioreactors operating during the production of therapeutic monoclonal antibodies.

### 2.0 Scope
Applies to all upstream operations utilizing Bioreactor Units BR-01 through BR-08 in Cleanroom Suites 201–206.

### 3.0 Critical Process Parameters (CPPs)
* **3.1 Temperature Control:**
  * Nominal Setpoint: `37.0 °C`
  * Action Control Range: `36.5 °C to 37.5 °C`
  * Critical Limit: Temperature must not exceed `38.5 °C` for greater than 10 continuous minutes during feed phases.
* **3.2 Dissolved Oxygen (DO):**
  * Target: `30.0% to 50.0%`
  * Minimum Alert Level: `< 20.0%` for > 15 minutes.
* **3.3 pH Control:**
  * Setpoint: `7.05 ± 0.10`

### 4.0 Excursion Handling & Classification
* **4.1 Minor Excursion:** Temperature drift between `37.6 °C and 38.0 °C` for under 15 minutes.
* **4.2 Major Excursion:** 
  * Any temperature elevation `> 38.5 °C` lasting greater than 10 minutes.
  * Any temperature excursion `> 39.0 °C` regardless of duration.
  * Immediate action: Notify Shift QA Lead within 1 hour; quarantine current batch in-process harvest.
* **4.3 Critical Excursion:** Temperature `> 40.0 °C` causing gross cellular lysis and batch termination.

### 5.0 Equipment & Sensor Calibration
* **5.1 Calibration Interval:** All primary RTD temperature probes (RTD-01 through RTD-08) and DO optical sensors must undergo NIST-traceable calibration every 90 calendar days.
* **5.2 Calibration Expiration Lockout:** Operations are prohibited from initiating a batch run on any vessel if sensor calibration is past its due date.
