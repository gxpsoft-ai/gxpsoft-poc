# QUALITY MANAGEMENT SYSTEM STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE
**Document ID:** SOP-QMS-015  
**Version:** 4.0  
**Effective Date:** 2026-01-15  
**Title:** Management of Deviations, Nonconformances, and Root Cause Analysis  
**Compliance:** FDA QMSR (21 CFR Part 820 / ISO 13485:2016), 21 CFR Part 11, EU GMP Annex 11  

---

### 1.0 Purpose & Policy
To define the mandatory lifecycle for identifying, documenting, containing, investigating, and resolving planned and unplanned deviations in GxP manufacturing.

### 2.0 Deviation Lifecycle & Timelines
* **2.1 Event Logging:** All quality events must be recorded in the QMS within 24 hours of occurrence.
* **2.2 Immediate Containment:** For Major and Critical deviations, immediate product containment/quarantine must be executed within 24 hours of discovery.
* **2.3 Investigation Phase:** Root Cause Analysis (RCA) using standard tools (5-Why, Ishikawa/Fishbone) must be completed within 30 calendar days.
* **2.4 Electronic Signatures:** In compliance with 21 CFR Part 11:
  * QA Investigator must sign as `AUTHORED`.
  * QA Manager must sign with meaning `APPROVED_ROOT_CAUSE`.
  * Automated systems / AI agents may propose classifications and drafts, but are strictly prohibited from applying an electronic signature or authorizing record closure.

### 3.0 Risk Classification Criteria
* **3.1 Minor:** An unexpected event with no impact on Critical Quality Attributes (CQAs), product safety, sterility, purity, or identity.
* **3.2 Major:** An event that potentially impacts product CQAs, involves a Critical Process Parameter (CPP) limit breach, or recurs within a 12-month period.
* **3.3 Critical:** An event that directly compromises patient safety, product sterility, or regulatory compliance.

### 4.0 Root Cause Categories
1. **Equipment / Instrumentation:** Sensor failure, calibration drift, seal rupture, mechanical failure.
2. **Procedure / Documentation:** Ambiguous instruction, missing check step.
3. **Personnel / Execution:** Operator fatigue, failure to follow SOP, training gap.
4. **Material / Supplier:** Out-of-spec raw material, lot inconsistency.
5. **Environmental:** Cleanroom HVAC excursion, power surge.
